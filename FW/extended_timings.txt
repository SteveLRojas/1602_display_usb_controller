I have one crappy old display which refuses to work with the normal timings.
Here are some timing settings that make it work:
#define ST7066_E_PULSE_TIM		1
#define ST7066_FN_SET_INIT0_TIM	8000
#define ST7066_FN_SET_INIT1_TIM	200
#define ST7066_CLEAR_DISP_TIM	3000
#define ST7066_RETURN_HOME_TIM	3000
#define ST7066_WRITE_DATA_TIM	80
#define ST7066_DEFAULT_CMD_TIM	80
